If you have been a victim of a cyber-crime or are affected by cyber-criminals please report it to us and we will help you.

Please make a report if you need help.

Report process

To make a report you must follow 3 steps:

Step-1

Fill out the form and the required fields correctly.

Step-2

Review the information in the report before submitting.
If there are any mistakes return to step-1 to correct them.

Step-3

The report will be sent to us and we will support you.
